Description / finding

AMP8 · Beckton sludge upgrade

Secondary digester gas-handling train — incomplete isolation verification before hot-works trial.

During the 18 Sep assurance visit to Beckton STW sludge complex (PKG-SLDG-03), the contractor commenced a planned flange-break trial on Digester D2 gas outlet without a completed LOTO verification sheet co-signed by TW Process Safety. Temporary blank flanges were fitted, but the upstream biogas compressor interlock bypass was still active and had not been recorded on the PTW.

This conflicts with TW Process Safety Standard PSS-04 (Biogas systems) and the AMP8 Beckton Construction Phase Plan §7.3. Residual risk of uncontrolled gas release into the MEICA gallery if the trial is repeated under current controls.

LOTO gap PTW incomplete Biogas / digester PSS-04 Hot works interface

Required action / proposed decision

Proposed decision for LNI Director

Approve with conditions: Suspend Digester D2 gas-train intrusive works until (1) full LOTO verification co-signed by TW Process Safety, (2) PTW re-issued with compressor interlock status recorded, and (3) toolbox talk completed with Tier-1 and MEICA subcontractor. Release HOLD-0087 only after evidence uploaded.

Alternative: Escalate to Area Process Safety Manager if contractor disputes isolation scope or if repeat finding within 30 days.
