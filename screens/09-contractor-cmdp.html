Company overview

Tier-1 · AMP8 framework

CMDP JV is the lead contractor for Oxford STW AMP8 major upgrade (primary / secondary treatment package), mobilised January 2025 under the Thames Water capital delivery framework. Joint venture delivering process civils and MEICA interfaces across the Thames Valley catchment.

Scope covers primary clarifier and secondary treatment train upgrades, sludge thickening interfaces, and site-wide MEICA renewals. Package lead Nina Okonkwo sits in the Oxford site office and reports into the TW Project Manager (Helen Briggs). Compliance currently amber (RAMS under review) — aligned with the Oxford RED stress board.
